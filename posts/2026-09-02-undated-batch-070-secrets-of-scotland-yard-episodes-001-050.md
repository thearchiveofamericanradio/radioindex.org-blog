Produced in London by **Harry Alan Towers** (Towers of London) and narrated with aristocratic gravity by distinguished English stage and screen star **Clive Brook**, *Secrets of Scotland Yard* was syndicated internationally beginning on Wednesday, January 5, 1949. Each episode dramatized authentic historical case files and forensic breakthroughs from the archives of New Scotland Yard and its famed Black Museum:
- **Foundational Forensics & Bodysnatching (Episodes 1–15)**: Clive Brook chronicles the reconstruction of murder victims from microscopic hyoid bone fragments, the landmark photographic superimposition technique that convicted Dr. Buck Ruxton in 1935, and the infamous 1828 Edinburgh murders of William Burke and William Hare.

- **Classic Manhunts & Medical Malpractice (Episodes 31–50)**: This cohort covers Bernard Spilsbury's forensic demonstration in the George Joseph Smith "Brides in the Bath" drownings, the 1946 seaside manhunt for serial murderer Neville Heath, the lethal morphia poisonings of Nottingham nursing home operator Dorothea Waddingham, and the celebrated 1919 Green Bicycle mystery of Ronald Vivian Light.
