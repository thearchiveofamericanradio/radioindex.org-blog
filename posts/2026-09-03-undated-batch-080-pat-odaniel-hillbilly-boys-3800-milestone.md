## Master Undated Resolution Pipeline (Batch 80 of 1,607) — The 3,800-Broadcast Milestone Reached

With the completion of **Batch 80**, the Master Undated Broadcast Resolution Pipeline has officially crossed the **3,800-Broadcast Resolution Milestone**, bringing the cumulative project total to **3,823 verified broadcasts** across 80 completed batches.

Launching our ninth major series, ***Pat O'Daniel and the Hillbilly Boys*** (`3926394e-520c-4cbe-86c4-017cb57626c5`), Batch 80 resolves the first **50 sequential daily broadcasts** (Programs 1 through 50). Originally conceived by Texas Governor Wilbert Lee "Pappy" O'Daniel to promote Hillbilly Flour across WBAP (Fort Worth), the Texas Quality Network, and border powerhouse XEPN, the daily commercial transcription broadcasts were fronted by his son **Pat O'Daniel ("Patty Boy")** upon Pappy's inauguration as Governor in January 1939.

Airing daily Monday through Friday across syndicated stations beginning on January 23, 1939, the program became a cultural phenomenon of early American roots music:
- **Hot Fiddle Breakdowns & Fiddle Teasers (Programs 1–20)**: Virtuoso fiddler Carroll Hubbard ("Little Caesar the Fiddle Teaser") and Mike O'Daniel dazzle listeners with blistering Texas reels including *"Hell on the Wabash"*, *"The Hog Trough Reel"*, and *"Sally Ann"*.
- **Western Swing & Hillbilly Flour (Programs 21–35)**: Lead vocalist Leon Huff ("The Texas Songbird") and steel guitarist Kermit Whalin deliver infectious renditions of pop swing standards like *"Dinah"* and *"I'm Getting Sentimental Over You"*, interspersed with cheerful flour plugs and down-home humor.
- **Novelty Songs & Border Blaster Broadcasts (Programs 36–50)**: Highlighting regional radio humor and string-band virtuosity, the band serves up listener favorites like *"The Tonsorial Parlor"* and *"Chewing Chewing Chewing Gum"*.

Reaching **3,823 broadcasts resolved** across the master project ledger, each entry features verified daily airdates, standardized Western swing taxonomy, and pure narrative synopses.
