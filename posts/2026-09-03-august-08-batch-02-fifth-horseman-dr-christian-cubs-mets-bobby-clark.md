### Key Historical & Archival Insights Extracted

1. **The Fifth Horseman's Prophetic Warning**: Broadcast exactly one year after the atomic bombing of Hiroshima on August 8, 1946, NBC's *The Fifth Horseman* starring Dane Clark represented American radio's most unflinching examination of the newly signed McMahon Atomic Energy Act and the existential dread of nuclear warfare.
2. **Shea Stadium Soundscape**: The August 8, 1965 Sunday broadcast of the New York Mets hosting the Chicago Cubs features the legendary broadcast triumvirate of Lindsey Nelson, Ralph Kiner, and Bob Murphy, interspersed with historic live Rheingold Beer commercial announcements.
3. **Dale Carnegie Meets Charlie McCarthy**: At the height of *How to Win Friends and Influence People* mania in the summer of 1937, Dale Carnegie appeared alongside Edgar Bergen and the wisecracking wooden dummy Charlie McCarthy on Sunday evening's highest-rated network broadcast.
