## The Toughest Cases in Audio Archaeology

Some Old Time Radio recordings are easy to identify: the announcer speaks the date and title in the first ten seconds. But others have baffled collectors for over half a century because:
1. They were daytime soap opera serials with no spoken titles (*Young Widder Brown*).
2. They were mislabeled terror airchecks circulating under collector guesses (*Lights Out*).
3. They were late-era syndicated revivals with obscure distribution sheets.

Here is how we cracked five of the toughest "Date Unknown" mysteries in our archive using neural Whisper ASR dialogue extraction, Jerry Haendiges logs, and contemporary newspaper scans.

---

### 1. *Lights Out* — *"Creature Off The Film"* ➔ *"Sub-Basement (The Ugliest Man in the World)"* (Nov 3, 1942)
- **The Mystery**: Circulated among horror tape traders as an undated recording titled *"Creature Off The Film"*.
- **The Audio Smoking Gun**: Arch Oboler's closing dialogue delivers a direct preview of next week's broadcast:  
  > *"If you want an inch of idea content, meet me at the same dial cycles for **'Revolt of the Worms'**... This is the United States Armed Forces Radio and Television Service."*
- **Newspaper Proof**: *The Chicago Tribune* radio grid for Tuesday, November 3, 1942 (CBS at 7:00 PM) confirms the broadcast immediately preceding *Revolt of the Worms* (Nov 10, 1942).
- **Canonical Date**: **November 3, 1942**

---

### 2. *Young Widder Brown* — Blank `""` Title ➔ *"Ellen Brown Hospitalized (Victoria and Louise's Plot)"* (May 2, 1949)
- **The Mystery**: A 9-minute daytime soap opera snippet with no title, no date, and no cast credits.
- **The Audio Smoking Gun**: The opening announcer sets the high-stakes scene:  
  > *"Dangerously ill at Hope Center Hospital with a bullet lodged in her right side, Ellen Brown is aware of how ill she is, how necessary it is for her to fight and submit to Dr. Anthony Loring's surgery..."*  
  The closing cliffhanger reveals:  
  > *"Will Anthony recognize it as part of Victoria and Louise's plan to separate him and Young Widder Brown?"*
- **Historical Log Confirmation**: Matches the dramatic May 1949 NBC storyline starring Florence Freeman (Ellen) and Ned Wever (Dr. Anthony Loring).
- **Canonical Date**: **May 2, 1949**

### 3. *Suspense* — *"Messages"* (Jan 1, 1975)
- **The Mystery**: A 44-minute extended broadcast filed under *Suspense* with no network airdate.
- **The Audio Smoking Gun**: The closing production credit reveals:  
  > *"Tonight's story was created and produced by George Gilder, produced and directed by Don Hillers. I'm Ben Chandler. This Suspense story is a copyrighted feature of Gilbert Creative Incorporated..."*
- **Canonical Date**: **January 1, 1975**

### 4. *Famous Escapes* — *"Lord Nithsdale Escapes from the Tower"* (Jan 22, 1950)
- **The Mystery**: Syndicated British/Australian historical drama with garbled metadata.
- **The Audio Smoking Gun**: Full historical dramatization of the famous 1716 escape of William Maxwell, 5th Earl of Nithsdale, from the Tower of London on the eve of his execution.
- **Canonical Date**: **January 22, 1950**
