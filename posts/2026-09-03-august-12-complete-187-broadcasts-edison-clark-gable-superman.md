### Key Historical & Archival Insights Extracted

1. **The 1961 M&M Boys (Mantle & Maris) Pennant Chase on Radio (August 12, 1961)**: Broadcast live from Griffith Stadium in Washington, D.C. over WPIX and the Yankee Radio Network, this game captures the height of the summer 1961 home run race. Announcers Mel Allen, Red Barber, and Phil Rizzuto call the action as Roger Maris (at 45 home runs) and Mickey Mantle (at 43) chase Babe Ruth's single-season record of 60 home runs, sponsored by Ballantine Beer and Camel Cigarettes.
2. **Ronald Reagan in Father Peyton's 'Family Theater' (August 12, 1948)**: Hosted by Lloyd Nolan under the motto *'The family that prays together stays together,'* this production stars 37-year-old Warner Bros. actor Ronald Reagan in *The Tin Whistle*. Reagan plays an embittered father whose hardened skepticism softens when a simple tin whistle brings him back to faith and family devotion.
3. **Dual Eras of 'The Adventures of Superman' (1946 & 1948)**: Bud Collyer's seamless vocal transition from the timid, mild-mannered Clark Kent to the resonant, authoritative Man of Steel is on full display across two storylines. In 1946's *The Secret Menace Strikes* (sponsored by Kellogg's Pep), Superman tackles radioactive sabotage, while 1948's *The Secret of the Genie* explores mystical antiquities in the Arabian desert.
4. **Real-Life Ballistics in 'True Detective Mysteries' (August 12, 1937)**: Drawn directly from the police archives of Macfadden's *True Detective Mysteries* magazine, this two-part broadcast details the forensic firearm investigation following the shooting of High Springs, Florida town official Bonnie Collins, demonstrating how early crime radio dramatized emerging forensic science years before *Dragnet*.
