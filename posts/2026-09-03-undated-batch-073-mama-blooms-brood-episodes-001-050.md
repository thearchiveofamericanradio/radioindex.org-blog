## Master Undated Resolution Pipeline (Batch 73 of 1,607) — *Mama Bloom's Brood* Enters the Pipeline

Following the complete restoration of six classic series, the master undated broadcast resolution pipeline introduces one of early radio's most delightful and underappreciated domestic comedy serials: ***Mama Bloom's Brood*** (`0a524245-870b-40e2-9da7-336c85e82f04`). **Batch 73** resolves the first **50 sequential daily broadcasts** (Episodes 1 through 50).

Recorded and syndicated nationally beginning on January 1, 1934, the 15-minute daily comedy stars **Lou Meril** as hard-working garment patriarch **Papa Jake Bloom** and comedy legend **Bea Benaderet** (in one of her earliest documented starring radio credits) as matriarch **Mama Becky Bloom**, with **Lindsay MacHarrie** announcing. Often described by vintage radio historians as a faster, more overtly humorous counterpart to *The Goldbergs*, the series chronicles the boisterous adventures of the Bloom family:
- **Weddings, Suitors, & Knee-Pants (Episodes 1–25)**: As daughters Yetta and Sara field suitors Sidney and Harold, Papa Jake balances the accounts of his thriving knee-pants factory, surviving frivolous lawsuits, gold-mine promotions, and extravagant Orchard Street bargain hunting for imported wedding gowns.
- **Retirement & The Western Exodus (Episodes 26–38)**: Following double family weddings, the children convince thirty-year industry veteran Jake that it is time to retire. Selling his factory building for eighty thousand dollars, Jake packs the touring car for an epic cross-country migration to sunny Southern California.
- **The Hollywood Mogul & Studio Chaos (Episodes 39–50)**: Arriving in Los Angeles, the Blooms tour motion picture lots, where Papa Jake is mistaken for a European film producer. Seizing the opportunity, Jake buys a controlling interest in an independent film company, appointing sons-in-law Sidney and Harold as studio managers while Mama Becky pacifies temperamental leading ladies with homemade chicken soup.
