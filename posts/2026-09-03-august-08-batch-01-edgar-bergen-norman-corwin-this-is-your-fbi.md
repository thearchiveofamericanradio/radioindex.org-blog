### Key Historical & Archival Insights Extracted

1. **Charlie McCarthy vs. Nelson Eddy**: In August 1937, Edgar Bergen and his wooden alter ego Charlie McCarthy ruled Sunday night radio ratings for Chase & Sanborn, here trading sharp comedic jibes with screen baritone idol Nelson Eddy.
2. **Norman Corwin's Wartime Humanism**: "A Very Fine Type Girl" exemplifies Norman Corwin's rare ability to weave lyrical radio poetry with grounded, everyday home-front experiences during the height of the Second World War.
3. **Pioneering 1929 Network Radio**: The preserved August 8, 1929 fragments of *Amos 'n' Andy* capture the nascent dawn of coast-to-coast commercial episodic radio under Pepsodent sponsorship.
