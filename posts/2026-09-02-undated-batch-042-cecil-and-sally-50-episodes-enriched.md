Airing daily across the West Coast on KPO/KYA and nationwide via transcription discs between March 9 and May 5, 1932, this sequence chronicles several of the program's most celebrated farcical storylines:
- **The Mrs. Mason & Dr. Gilroy Wedding Debacle**: After Mamie learns Garrett Gilroy has kept his seven children secret and flees, predatory widow Mrs. Mason announces a whirlwind engagement to the doctor. Sally agrees to sing a solo soprano hymn at the ceremony, while Cecil tries to rent a tuxedo from a bewildered neighborhood butcher. At the altar, Dr. Gilroy is a no-show, leaving behind a blunt desertion note, and Mrs. Mason pays hush money to Cecil and Sally to keep the humiliating scene quiet.

- **Nero Eats the Thirty Dollars**: Sally entrusts Cecil with thirty dollars of charity club dues. Disaster strikes when Cecil's rambunctious dog Nero literally chews up and swallows the cash.
- **The Warehouse Accusation**: Desperate to replace the devoured charity funds, Cecil rents his jalopy *Ben Hur* to a waterfront character and borrows pocket money from Uncle Thomas' store register. Rival Alexander witnesses the transaction, accuses Cecil of theft and bootlegging, and provokes Cecil into challenging him to a bare-knuckle boxing match.
