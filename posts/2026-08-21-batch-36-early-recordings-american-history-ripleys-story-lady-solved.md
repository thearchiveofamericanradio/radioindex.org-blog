## Batch 36 Forensic Discoveries

Batch 36 resolves 32 acoustic discs and radio master broadcasts: 8 pre-1920 acoustic 78 RPM recordings from ***Early Recordings*** (featuring Billy Murray, Ada Jones, Alma Gluck, Henry Burr, and the Original Dixieland Jazz Band), 8 historical radio documentary dramas and speeches from ***American History Through The Eyes Of Radio*** (including DuPont's *Cavalcade of America*, CBS's *You Are There*, President Truman's 1945 peace address, and live Apollo 11 moon landing audio), 8 syndicated sixty-second curiosities from ***Ripley's Believe It or Not - One Minute Episodes*** (Spring 1948), and 8 children's radio fables from ***The Story Lady*** (Summer 1949).
