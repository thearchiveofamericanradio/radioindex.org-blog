## Batch 46 Forensic Discoveries

Batch 46 resolves 26 landmark radio broadcasts: 8 personal philosophy broadcasts from Edward R. Murrow's acclaimed ***This I Believe*** (highlighted by former U.S. President Harry S. Truman stating his life creed, UCLA Chancellor Raymond B. Allen, Italian Renaissance art scholar Bernard Berenson, and 20th Century Limited locomotive engineer William B. Thompson), 8 sequential 1931 NBC Pacific episodes from ***Cecil and Sally***, 8 Armed Forces Radio Service transcription discs from the ***AFRS Basic Music Library*** (featuring Sam Cooke live at the Copacabana, Louis Jordan's Tympany Five, and Bob Wills' Texas Playboys), and 2 sequential dramatic chapters from the daytime serial ***Aunt Mary***.
