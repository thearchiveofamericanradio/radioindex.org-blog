### Key Historical & Archival Insights Extracted

1. **Charles Russell's Johnny Dollar Era**: Before Bob Bailey redefined the character in multi-part weeknight serials, Charles Russell established Johnny Dollar as a tough-talking, wry, expense-account-conscious investigator with a heart of gold.
2. **Frank Lovejoy's Gritty Chicago Noir**: Frank Lovejoy brought an authentic, world-weary newsman's cadence to *Nightbeat*, evoking late-night neon diners, rainy gutters, and human empathy for society's downtrodden.
3. **Late-Run Gunsmoke Integrity**: Even in August 1960 as network radio was being phased out in favor of television, the CBS radio cast of *Gunsmoke*—William Conrad, Parley Baer, Howard McNear, and Georgia Ellis—delivered uncompromising, mature frontier drama under director Norman Macdonnell.
