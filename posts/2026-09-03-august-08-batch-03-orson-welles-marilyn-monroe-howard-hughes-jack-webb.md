### Key Historical & Archival Insights Extracted

1. **Marilyn Monroe's Immediate Radio Eulogy**: Transmitted on Wednesday, August 8, 1962, just days after her death on August 4/5, NBC's *Fame Is Fickle* special captured the poignant dissonance between Monroe's sex-symbol celebrity and her profound, earnest desire to be treated as a serious dramatic artist.
2. **Howard Hughes vs. Owen Brewster**: The August 8, 1947 ABC live remote coverage captures the fiery climax of Howard Hughes's Senate testimony, in which he famously turned the tables on Committee Chairman Owen Brewster, accusing him of blackmail on behalf of Pan American World Airways.
3. **Orson Welles's Mercury Literary Trio**: On August 8, 1938, months before the infamous *War of the Worlds* broadcast, Orson Welles showcased the Mercury Theatre's artistic range with a tripartite bill bridging Sherwood Anderson's Midwestern realism with Saki's British gothic wit.
