### A. The Screen Guild Theater: Joan Crawford and Ronald Colman in "None Shall Part Us"
Archival broadcast `7c817442` preserves the Gulf Screen Guild Show's October 15, 1939 presentation of "None Shall Part Us". Hosted by Roger Pryor and announced by John Conte, this half-hour romantic wartime drama united Hollywood legends Joan Crawford (Sarah Farrington), Ronald Colman (Captain Alan Harcourt), Lew Ayres (Michael Farrington), and Montague Love (Sir George Packman) in support of the Motion Picture Relief Fund. Contemporary radio timetables in the *Los Angeles Times* confirm the broadcast time and network distribution over CBS.
