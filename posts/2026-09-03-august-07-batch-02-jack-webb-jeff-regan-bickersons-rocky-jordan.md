### Key Historical & Archival Insights Extracted

1. **Pre-Dragnet Jack Webb**: Before revolutionizing police procedurals with *Dragnet*, Jack Webb defined hardboiled West Coast detective radio as Jeff Regan ("The Lion's Eye"), delivering cynical, machine-gun-paced dialogue under director William N. Robson.
2. **The Comedy of Insomnia & Bickering**: Philip Rapp's razor-sharp scripting for *The Bickersons* set a high-water mark for mid-century domestic satire, pitting Don Ameche's exhausted, rasping baritone against Frances Langford's shrill, relentless late-night cross-examinations.
3. **Frank Sinatra on Your Hit Parade**: In August 1948, Frank Sinatra was headlining *Your Hit Parade* for Lucky Strike, bringing his lush vocal phrasing to Cole Porter's "Just One of Those Things" during the show's golden late-1940s run.
4. **Douglas Miller's Pre-War Warnings**: In August 1941, four months prior to Pearl Harbor, NBC's *Speaking of Liberty* served as a critical platform for Douglas Miller and mystery author Rex Stout to expose the economic reality of totalitarian expansionism to American radio listeners.
