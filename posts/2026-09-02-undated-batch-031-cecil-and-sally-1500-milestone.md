## Master Undated Resolution Pipeline (Batch 31 of 1,607) — *1,500 Broadcasts Resolved Milestone*

With the completion of **Batch 31** (50 broadcasts, Sequences 488 through 537), the master undated broadcast resolution project achieves a momentous benchmark: **1,539 broadcasts fully dated, titled, and resolved across 31 consecutive cohorts**.

Focusing on the historic daily transcribed comedy serial ***Cecil and Sally*** (starring **Johnny Patrick** and **Helen Troy**), this cohort resolves Episodes 131 through 180, charting the daily broadcast continuum from June 6, 1930 to August 2, 1930.

This summer sequence contains some of the funniest set-pieces in early broadcasting:
- **The Loose Tooth Crisis**: Cecil whines about an aching tooth until a massive sneeze launches it across the parlor.
- **The Masquerade Drag Saga**: Cecil loses a bet and allows Sally to dress him in petticoats and high heels for a costume ball, where he gets into a fistfight defending Sally's honor and later runs out of gas in his jalopy *Ben Hur*, forcing them to siphon gas from a farmer who mistakes Cecil for Sally's sister.
- **Chewing Tobacco Bravado**: Seeking to project rugged masculinity, Cecil tries a plug of tobacco and spends days nursing severe sickness on the divan.
- **The Park Statue Debacle**: Cecil climbs a civic monument to impress Sally, gets stuck, and watches the responding police officer climb up and get stranded alongside him.
