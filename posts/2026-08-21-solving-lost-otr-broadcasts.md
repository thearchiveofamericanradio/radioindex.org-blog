## The 76,000-Recording Challenge

In Old Time Radio (OTR) preservation, collector tapes have circulated for over half a century labeled with vague tags like *"Date Unknown"*, *"The Part 19"*, or completely blank titles. Across the 273,234 recordings in the radioindex source-of-record archive, **76,321 recordings** entered our catalog without verified calendar airdates.

Today, we established a deterministic 4-step discovery pipeline combining **local Whisper ASR audio extraction** with **Jerry Haendiges (`jjonez`) master radio logs**, **J. David Goldin's database**, and **contemporary newspaper archives** to begin systematically solving these mysteries.

## First Solved Discoveries

### 1. *The Whistler* — Recovered Title: *"Five Cent Call"* (Feb 19, 1950)
- **Original Tape Label**: `"The Part 19"` (Undated)
- **Forensic Audio Discovery**: Announcer Marvin Miller introduces: *"And now for the Signal Oil Company, The Whistler's strange story: 'Five Cent Call'."*
- **Primary Source Match**: Jerry Haendiges CBS master log Episode #403; confirmed cast featuring Gerald Mohr, Virginia Gregg, and Betty Lou Gerson.
- **Canonical Date**: **February 19, 1950**

### 2. *The Whistler* — Recovered Title: *"Stranger in the House"* (June 2, 1948)
- **Original Tape Label**: `"The Part 14"` (Undated)
- **Forensic Audio Discovery**: Spoken story title *"Stranger in the House"*, starring Virginia Gregg as Helen awaiting her foster brother Ted from the Orient.
- **Primary Source Match**: `jjonez` CBS Wednesday summer rebroadcast log (Orig. Sept 2, 1946, Ep #223).
- **Canonical Date**: **June 2, 1948**

### 3. *The Shadow* — *"The Man Who Murdered Time"* (Jan 1, 1939)

- **Primary Source Match**: Mutual/WOR master log for New Year's Day 1939 with Bill Johnstone as The Shadow.
- **Canonical Date**: **January 1, 1939**
