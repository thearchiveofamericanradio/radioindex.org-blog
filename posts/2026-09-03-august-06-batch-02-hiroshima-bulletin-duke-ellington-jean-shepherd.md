### Key Historical & Archival Insights Extracted

1. **August 6, 1945 Historic Broadcast**: The network special report capturing Eben Ayers reading President Truman's announcement represents one of the most consequential news bulletins in broadcasting history, marking the dawn of the atomic age.
2. **Luise Rainer's Radio Debut (1936)**: Austrian-American actress Luise Rainer—who would go on to win back-to-back Academy Awards for *The Great Ziegfeld* (1936) and *The Good Earth* (1937)—made her American radio dramatic debut on the Fleischmann Yeast Hour on this date.
3. **Duke Ellington's Legendary Chicago Residency**: NBC's late-night remote pickups from Frank Holzfeind's Blue Note jazz club captured the Ellington band at peak mid-century form, documented in contemporary *Chicago Tribune* night club columns.
4. **Brown Derby Archival Lore**: Chuck Schaden's interview with Betty Lou Gerson recorded on-location inside the Hollywood Brown Derby captures firsthand recollections of Golden Age Chicago radio production during the 1930s and 1940s.
