## Master Undated Resolution Pipeline (Batch 56 of 1,607) — *The Planet Man* Galactic Odyssey Begins

Following the 100% resolution of *This I Believe* and *Cecil and Sally*, the master undated resolution pipeline pivots to classic mid-century juvenile science fiction with ***The Planet Man*** (`8b3be765-ce7f-4390-b054-d3a9e33d4c74`). **Batch 56** resolves the first **50 sequential daily broadcasts** (Episodes 1 through 50).

Produced by Palladium Radio Productions in New York and syndicated Monday through Friday to radio stations nationwide throughout 1950, *The Planet Man* stars **Jack Grimes** as **Dantro**, the interstellar troubleshooter for the League of Planets. Piloting the advanced rocket cruiser *Planeteer*, Dantro is accompanied by Earth scientist Dr. John Darrow, his spirited daughter Pat, youthful siblings Billy and Jane, and crusty flight engineer Slats.

This opening cohort covers broadcasts from January 2 to March 10, 1950, introducing listeners to a sprawling interplanetary cosmos:
- **The Lunar Reconnaissance & The Martian Menace (Episodes 1–12)**: Dantro tests the subspace cosmic communicator in lunar orbit before intercepting war transmissions from evil Martian dictator Marston. Marston dispatches commandos into underground lunar catacombs to kidnap young Billy and Jane, leading Dantro on a perilous rescue across the asteroid belt.
- **The Dusters of Venus (Episodes 13–20)**: Entering the corrosive sulfur storms of Venus, the crew is captured by nomadic desert tribesmen known as the Dusters and imprisoned beneath the Venusian citadel, where Dantro unmasks a corrupt planetary minister conspiring with Martian invaders.
- **The Asteroid Uranium Pirates (Episodes 21–33)**: Interstellar outlaws Slick and Blackie encircle the *Planeteer* with magnetic proximity mines to seize its League propulsion drive. Stranded on Asteroid 47, Billy and Jane are sheltered by an eccentric prospector named Rocks before discovering rich veins of fissionable uranium.
- **Mind Control & The Libra Sector (Episodes 34–50)**: Marston beams a hypnotic carrier frequency into Dantro's communicator, nearly compelling him to surrender the ship until Slats severs the hull transponder. Seeking advanced defense technology to counter Marston's orbital weapons, Dantro navigates toward the uncharted Libra star system, braving sulfur-breathing aliens and encountering holographic emissaries from the spire city of Mardee.
