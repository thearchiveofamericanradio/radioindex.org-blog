## Master Undated Resolution Pipeline (Batch 53 of 1,607) — *Cecil and Sally* Million-Dollar Will Arc

Continuing our chronological resolution across the 1928–1933 syndicated daily comedy serial ***Cecil and Sally*** (starring **Johnny Patrick** and **Helen Troy**), **Batch 53** resolves an additional **50 sequential broadcasts** (Sequences 1588 through 1637, representing Episodes 1231 through 1280).

Airing daily across the West Coast on KPO/KYA and nationwide via transcription discs between December 11, 1933 and February 6, 1934, this winter storyline moves from legal exoneration to eccentric domestic comedy:
- **Exoneration & Lemon Pie**: Recaptured after his armed flight, Cecil faces a tense murder trial for the death of boarder Dr. Milton. When a surprise witness confesses, Cecil is cleared of all charges and welcomed home by Sally with a homemade lemon meringue pie.
- **The Million-Dollar Bequest & Alexander's Guardianship**: Word arrives from abroad that Aunt Violet has passed away, bequeathing Cecil an astronomical one million dollars on the strict condition that he and Sally remain continuously and happily married. Horror strikes when probate court names archnemesis Alexander as the estate's sole legal executor and guardian.
- **Susie Saddle the Kleptomaniac Maid**: After being sentenced in night court for hawking newspapers without a vendor's license, magistrate Judge Horton remands reformed kleptomaniac Susie Saddle to their home as a domestic maid. Susie promptly makes off with Cecil's shirts and the dinner silverware.
- **Baby Bud Reunited With His Grandfather**: Hosting Judge Horton for Sunday dinner, Cecil and Sally hear the tragic story of the magistrate's runaway daughter and lost grandson. Matching baby clothes and birthmarks, the couple discover that baby Bud is Judge Horton's grandson and tearfully surrender him to his family.
- **Little Otis Takes Over**: Aunt Bess arranges for energetic ten-year-old Otis to board with them, sparking chaotic escapades involving runaway newspaper routes, black eyes, tied door handles, and castor oil cures.
