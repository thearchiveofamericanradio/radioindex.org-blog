### Critical Forensics & Misattribution Corrections

1. **Spoken Date Authentication in News Broadcasts (`1307f0e2`)**: Circulating catalogs had tagged a CBS war broadcast as August 6, 1940. Acoustic transcription revealed Bob Trout explicitly opening: *"Wednesday, August 14th, there was a little air activity over Britain today."* The record was corrected to its authentic airdate of August 14, 1940.
2. **Commercial Slogans and Sponsor Archaeology**: Spoken audio preserved the classic General Mills radio slogan: *"General Mills, makers of Cheerios, the oat cereal ready to eat, and Wheaties, Breakfast of Champions, presents The Lone Ranger!"*
3. **Information Please Cultural Landmark**: The August 6, 1950 broadcast preserved an extraordinary literary panel: author James Michener appearing shortly after winning the Pulitzer Prize for *Tales of the South Pacific*, trading wits with Cornelia Otis Skinner and John Kieran.
