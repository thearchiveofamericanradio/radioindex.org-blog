## Batch 84 Forensic Discoveries

Batch 84 recovers and dates 15 historical broadcasts spanning classic BBC Radio literary adaptations, symphonic concert hours, country music heritage milestones, pre-Code Hollywood musical previews, foundational 1926 Chicago radio serials, and big band recruitment programs: 3 celebrated BBC Radio 4 readings of Roald Dahl macabre masterpieces on ***Roald Dahl Short Stories*** (featuring Patricia Routledge, Joanna David, and Jenny Agutter, 1990), 3 symphonic salon broadcasts conducted by Dr. Frank Black on ***The Cadillac Symphony Orchestra*** (NBC, 1951), 3 rare national radio spot announcements inaugurating the country music industry's first official celebration on ***National Country Music Week 1961*** (featuring Tex Ritter, Buck Owens, and George Hamilton IV), 2 preview chapters for Busby Berkeley's musical *Gold Diggers of 1935* on ***Direct from Hollywood***, 2 historic May 1926 78 RPM broadcast discs from Freeman Gosden and Charles Correll on ***Sam and Henry*** (WGN Chicago), and Les Brown's Band of Renown on ***The Les Brown Show*** (1953).
