## Batch 7 Forensic Discoveries

Batch 7 resolves 18 historical syndicated electrical transcription broadcasts across Goodman & Jane Ace's legendary comedy run of ***Easy Aces*** (1935–1936 NBC/CBS syndication master discs, cataloged via Terry Salomonson and Martin Grams master indices) and the 1946 anthology run of ***Strange Adventure***.
