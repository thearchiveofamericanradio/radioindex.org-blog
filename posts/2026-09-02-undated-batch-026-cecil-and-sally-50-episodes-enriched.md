## Master Undated Resolution Pipeline (Batch 26 of 1,607) — *Cecil and Sally* Inauguration

Following the 100% completion of Edward R. Murrow's *This I Believe* across Batches 1–25 (1,239 broadcasts), **Stage 1** expands to the single largest undated collection in the archive: ***Cecil and Sally*** (also known as *The Private Life of Cecil and Sally*).

Created by and starring **Johnny Patrick** (as Cecil) and **Helen Troy** (as Sally), this pioneering daily comedy serial began in 1928 on San Francisco's KYA and KPO, rapidly expanding via electrical transcriptions across North America to an estimated daily audience of 15 million listeners. Preceding even *Amos 'n' Andy* as a daily transcribed narrative comedy, the show followed the comical domestic misadventures, romantic rivalries, and suburban squabbles of Cecil, Sally, Uncle Thomas, Aunt Bess, and the formidable Mrs. Mason.

**Batch 26** resolves **50 broadcasts** (Sequences 232 through 287), restoring clean canonical episode titles, verified broadcast airdates across the 1930–1933 schedule, standardized comedy taxonomy, and narrative synopses.
