Airing daily across the West Coast on KPO/KYA and nationwide via transcription discs between April 21 and June 17, 1933, this colorful sequence blends thrilling detective adventure with classic slapstick neighborhood farces:
- **The Mausoleum Crypt Rescue**: Cecil and Sally slip into the foggy cemetery at midnight with a flashlight, unearthing Willie's captive father tied up in a stone crypt. Police detectives rush the mausoleum just as the counterfeit ring returns, securing the stolen printing plates and awarding Cecil and Sally a federal informant cash bounty.
- **The Lakefront Hamburger Stand Washout**: Flush with federal reward money, Cecil and Sally open an outdoor hamburger and soda concession by the park lake. After three customer-less hours, they serve their very first burger to a passing vagrant free of charge, only for a torrential Sunday downpour to flood the grounds and wash away the canvas stand.
- **The Rose Garden Billy Goat Siege**: Sally devises an absurd plot to force Mrs. Mason to move away by renting a foul-tempered billy goat. The plan backfires spectacularly when the bucking goat charges the neighborhood, forcing Cecil, Sally, and Mrs. Mason to scramble up the same apple tree.
- **Sally as Joan of Arc**: Cecil takes a hauling job transporting a massive workhorse named Ichabod. Sally transcribes the wrong address, stranding them with the hungry beast until they meet flamboyant French theatrical impresario Pierre. Pierre casts Sally as Joan of Arc in his traveling tent show, only for an errant stage torch to set the canvas roof ablaze during the opening act.
